# SAWT Brand Identity

## 1. Brand Philosophy
The SAWT brand embodies creativity and excellence in gaming, focusing on empowering players and fostering a vibrant community.

## 2. Complete Color System
- **Primary Color**: Black 
  - HEX: #000000
- **Secondary Color**: Gold 
  - HEX: #FFD700
- **Accent Colors**: 
  - Dark Gold: #B8860B
  - Light Gold: #FAEBD7

## 3. Typography Specifications
- **Headings**: Montserrat Bold
- **Body Text**: Open Sans Regular
- **Call to Action**: Montserrat Semi-Bold

## 4. App Screen Designs
- **Homepage**: Minimalist layout with a focus on user engagement.
- **Profile Page**: Personalized player stats and achievements feature prominently.

## 5. Talent Level System
- Levels are depicted visually as tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum with distinct designs for each level.

## 6. Subscription Tiers with Visual Hierarchy
- **Free Tier**: Basic access with limited features.
- **Gold Tier**: Enhanced features and monthly perks. 
- **Platinum Tier**: All access with exclusive rewards.

## 7. Challenge Interface
A user-friendly interface that presents challenges in an engaging manner, with progress bars and visual indicators of completion.

## 8. Intro Video Specifications
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 pixels
- **Duration**: 1 minute and 30 seconds
- **Content**: Engaging introduction to the brand and game mechanics.

## 9. Executive Messaging Language
- Use of inclusive language that emphasizes community and adventure.

## 10. Design Guidelines for All UI Components
- Consistency in color and typography.
- Use of whitespace to enhance readability.
- Button styles should be clearly defined with hover effects to enhance user experience.
